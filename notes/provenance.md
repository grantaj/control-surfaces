# Provenance

Control Surfaces grew from work on [`grantaj/censorship`](https://github.com/grantaj/censorship), a descriptive taxonomy and structural account of censorship.

The originating observation was that operations described there also map naturally onto actions available to an artist: exclusion can become omission or erasure; attenuation can become demotion, delay, friction, or containment; transformation can become modification or substitution; saturation can become repetition, diversion, or overload; anticipatory restraint can become self-imposed limits. Later mining extended beyond those categories into selection, status, precedent, defaults, memory, irreversibility, and other artistic provocations.

This relationship is developmental provenance, not a constraint on the deck. The academic taxonomy is not intended to appear on the cards, determine their organisation, or serve as a fidelity test. Once an operation produces a useful artistic prompt, artistic usefulness takes precedence over conceptual correspondence.

The raw mining passes are retained so that later editing can be aggressive without losing the history of how candidate prompts emerged.
