# Control Surfaces

A deck of prompts for artistic practice.

The prompts are short interventions intended to disturb an emerging work: remove, delay, substitute, obscure, repeat, constrain, reclassify, preserve, forget, reopen, or otherwise alter what the work is allowed to become.

The project began from an observation made while developing a structural taxonomy of censorship: many operations in that taxonomy translate naturally into artistic actions. The deck does not teach or illustrate that taxonomy. The taxonomy is a generative source; the cards are judged by whether they provoke interesting artistic decisions.

## Development

- `source/` preserves raw mining passes before editorial compression.
- `cards/candidates.md` is the working pool for prompts that survive into deck consideration.
- `notes/provenance.md` records the project's conceptual origin and keeps that machinery out of the cards themselves.

The next stage is editorial: collapse repeated actions, remove weak prompts, and retain a compact set that produces genuinely different moves in practice.
